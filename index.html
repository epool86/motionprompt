How it works

Three steps. No editing app.

Claude writes the animation as a web page. Your browser plays it, and your screen recorder turns it into a video file.

  1. 01

    Pick a style

    Browse live demos and find a look you like: bold text, glowing particles, a spinning 3D logo.

  2. 02

    Copy the prompt

    Every style comes with a prompt. Change the words and colours, then paste it into Claude.

  3. 03

    Record and post

    Open the page Claude gives you, record the screen, and share it anywhere you post videos.
