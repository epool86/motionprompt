Make my video in this style: Editorial portfolio.

Look and motion
- A luxury fashion and design magazine about me, shot on a dark desk. It opens on the cover, then the pages turn one by one like a real magazine.

- Use my portrait and project images if I attach them (a portrait photo and two to four images of my work). If I don't, draw tasteful stand ins in a flat editorial illustration style.
- Fit the shape of the video: a single tall page for vertical videos, open two page spreads for wide videos, and a page lying at an angle on the desk for square videos.

Type and colour

- Music: a stylish slow jazz bed at about 100 bpm: soft electric piano chords, a walking upright bass, brushed snare and a light ride cymbal, kept low under the effects and looping cleanly.
